Questions

Good to know.

Does Tornade stream music?

No. Tornade plays the files you own, on your Mac, an external drive or a network share.

Do I need an account?

No. Download, point Tornade at your folders, press play.

Where is my data stored?

Locally, in a SQLite database on your machine: library, playlists, ratings and history.

Can I edit tags and artwork?

Yes. FLAC tags and artwork, directly in the app.

When are Windows and Linux coming?

Both native apps are in development. The terminal edition, free and open source, is available today.
